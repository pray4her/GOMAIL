After evaluating several approaches, the **Two-Pass Distribution (方案一)** is the most suitable solution. It effectively resolves the email dropping issue while maintaining the existing code structure and minimizing complexity.

**Approach Details:**
1.  **First Pass (Initial Assignment):**
    - Perform an initial assignment pass, similar to the current logic.
    - Instead of discarding the unassigned emails when an account hits its limit, calculate the shortfall for each account.
    - Sum these shortfalls to get a 	otalUnassigned amount.

2.  **Second Pass (Redistribution):**
    - After the first pass, iterate **only** through the senders that still have remaining capacity (dailyRemain - assigned > 0).
    - Calculate the total remaining capacity across all these capable senders.
    - Distribute the 	otalUnassigned emails among these capable senders, proportionally based on their remaining capacity.

This approach ensures that the system's full sending potential is utilized before capping the total number of emails sent.
